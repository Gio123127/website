Typography (why): Fraunces for headings is a "soft serif" — old-style warmth with a little characterful quirk in its curves, so it feels hand-considered and editorial. That's exactly the boutique, one-artist story, while staying perfectly legible at display size (lean on the italic for warmth in subheads). Mulish for body is a clean humanist sans that's highly readable on a phone and stays out of Fraunces' way. Personality up top, calm clarity in the copy. Both are free on Google Fonts.
Imagery style: Use Gulshan's real work — her Instagram library is the asset, and macro close-ups of finished sets sell the craftsmanship better than any copy. SECTOR DOs AND DON'Ts
Do:

Show real prices openly on the page. Most NZ rivals — Anna's Beauty Lounge included — tuck their service list and pricing behind PDF downloads or a Fresha funnel. On-page transparency removes friction and reads as honest. Anna
Make booking one tap from mobile (sticky call / DM / Fresha button). It matches how clients actually arrive: Instagram, on a phone.
Lead with real work and a real face. The gallery and a Gulshan portrait do the selling; add brief aftercare notes to echo the "she teaches you how to look after them" theme from her reviews.
Put the verified 4.9★ / ~90-review proof up front and link to Google.

Don't:

Don't run the generic script. Competitors across the country lean on the same words — "luxurious," "relaxing," "indulge," "pampered," a "haven" or "retreat", and Anna's opens with a "luxurious beauty sanctuary" and a "journey to timeless beauty." Sounding identical is the trap; the warm, personal, honest angle is the whole differentiator. Nail Corner Studio + 2
Don't oversell "luxury spa" in a way that clashes with the genuine home-studio reality. Private, personal and unhurried is the stronger and truer story — lean in rather than paper over it.
Don't publish the full home address prominently. It's a private residence and clients value the discretion; use suburb plus "address on your booking confirmation."
Don't build a heavy slider-and-autoplay site. Keep it fast and calm for mobile and data connections — no autoplay music or video walls.
